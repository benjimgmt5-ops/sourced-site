Hero cars, delivered to set

The car is often the star of the shot. Sourced. supplies supercars, luxury SUVs, classics and picture vehicles for music videos, adverts, film and fashion shoots across London, delivered to your location on the day.

Our current list includes the Lamborghini Aventador, Huracán and a full colour range of Urus and Urus Performante, the Ferrari 458 Italia, 488 GTB and Purosangue, Rolls-Royce Cullinans including Black Badge, Bentley Bentayga, Brabus and AMG G-Wagons, Defenders and Escalades. Need several of the same model in different colours? We can line up a fleet.

Questions

Can the cars be driven on camera?

Often, yes, depending on the car and the shot. Tell us what the scene involves and we'll confirm what's possible for each vehicle.

Do you deliver the cars to set?

Yes. Cars are delivered to your location in London and the surrounding areas. Delivery is included in your quote.

Can I book several cars in matching or different colours?

Yes. We can line up fleets, for example several Urus Performantes in orange, red, yellow and green.

Do you supply chauffeurs?

Yes. Our chauffeur cars, including the Rolls-Royce Phantom and Maybachs, come with drivers for talent arrivals and moves between locations.
