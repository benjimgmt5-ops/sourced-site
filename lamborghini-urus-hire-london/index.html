About the Lamborghini Urus

The Urus is Lamborghini's super SUV, and one of the most requested hero cars for music videos and fashion shoots. It has the presence of a supercar with room for four, so it works for driving shots, arrivals and talent moves on the same day.

Our list covers the Urus in gloss black, white with a red interior, grey, matte grey and blue, plus the sharper Urus Performante in green, orange, yellow and red. Need a statement line-up? The Performantes can be booked together as a colour fleet.
