About the Ferrari

A Ferrari brings instant heritage to a shot. Our list covers three very different cars. The 458 Italia, in yellow with a black sport interior, is a classic mid-engined V8 shape. The 488 GTB, in rosso with a tan interior, is its turbocharged successor. The Purosangue, in rosso with a black sport interior, is Ferrari's four-door, four-seat car, so talent can actually ride in it on camera.

All three are delivered to set, and can be booked alongside Lamborghinis for a supercar line-up.
