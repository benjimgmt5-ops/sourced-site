About the Mercedes V-Class

A VIP V-Class is a private lounge on wheels: big enough for an artist and a few of their team, and comfortable enough to use as a mobile green room between locations.

Our list covers a two-tone white and black Mercedes-Maybach-style V-Class with a starlight lounge and screen for up to five passengers, a black Maybach-style V-Class, a V-Class with an orange leather lounge, curtains and starlight roof, one with a cream quilted interior and three-seat rear bench, and one with a white leather blue starlight lounge. All come with a driver.
