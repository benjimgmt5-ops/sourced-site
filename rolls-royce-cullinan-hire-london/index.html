About the Rolls-Royce Cullinan

The Cullinan is Rolls-Royce's SUV, and it has become a music video staple: the presence of a Rolls with a raised, commanding stance. The Black Badge version adds a darker, more aggressive look.

Our list covers the Black Badge Cullinan in black with a red interior, plus Cullinans in white with black wheels, blue and white two-tone, black with orange leather, silver with orange, and grey. They work as hero cars on camera and as chauffeur cars for talent between locations.
