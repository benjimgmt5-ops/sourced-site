For homeowners

Thinking about opening your home to shoots for the first time? Here's how we look after it. List your house here.

You decide everything

You set your day rate, choose your dates and approve every booking. You can turn down any shoot you're not comfortable with, with no penalty.

Insured productions only

Every production must show us valid public liability insurance before we confirm a booking. A damage deposit can be agreed for every shoot.

Security on site

We can put a professional security team on site for the whole booking, so your home is protected from arrival to wrap.

Agreed rules, in writing

Rooms that are off limits, crew numbers, parking, start and finish times: everything is agreed and confirmed before anyone arrives.

Protected floors and walls

Productions protect floors, doorways and furniture, and any furniture that's moved is put back where it was.

Paid properly

Payment terms are agreed up front, so you know exactly what you're getting before the shoot day.

What happens on shoot day

1. You're met by a Sourced. agent

Your dedicated Sourced. agent arrives before the crew, walks the house with you and confirms the rules for the day.

2. We run the whole booking

Your agent manages the production from start to finish: arrivals, parking, crew, deliveries and timings. You don't have to do anything, and you're welcome to stay or head out for the day.

3. Your home is protected throughout

Your agent checks floor and wall protection, keeps the crew to the agreed areas and deals with any questions so they never land on you.

4. Returned exactly as we received it

At wrap, your agent does a full walkthrough with you. Your location is returned exactly how it was received, and anything that isn't right is put right.
