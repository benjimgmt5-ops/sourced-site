The right house for the shot

Finding a location that looks right on camera, allows filming and is free on your date usually takes days of calls. Sourced. does that work for you. Tell us the look, the date and the size of your crew, and we come back with houses that fit, along with the cars, transport and security you need, in one quote.

Our current list covers stone and glass houses with indoor-outdoor pools, mansions with screening rooms and gardens, courtyard villas with indoor pools and bars, hillside houses with terraces and hot tubs, and gated family homes with cinemas and gyms, from Greater London out to Rickmansworth and Radlett. If what you need isn't listed, we find it.

How it works

Send a brief with your shoot date, location area, crew size and the look you're after. You can add specific houses from our listings, or leave it open. We check availability and filming terms, then send one quote that covers the location and anything else you've asked for. Nothing is booked until you accept.

Questions

Where are your locations?

Mostly London and the Home Counties, including Greater London, Rickmansworth and Radlett. We can source further afield across the UK on request.

Can I book a location for one day?

Yes. Most shoots are booked by the day or half day. Tell us your hours and crew size and we'll price it.

Can you also sort cars, transport and security for the same shoot?

Yes. That's the point of Sourced. One brief covers the location, picture cars, crew transport, security and anything else, with one quote.

How quickly can you find a house?

Send the brief as early as you can. We'll tell you straight away what's realistic for your date.
